IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ROUNDS FLOW:
  Tournament Detail Page
    ↓
  tournament-command-center.tsx (calls getRoundsByTournament)
    ↓
  TournamentService.getRoundsByTournament()
    ↓
  RoundRepository.getByTournament(tournamentId)
    ↓
  Returns 1-5 rounds per tournament
    ↓
  TournamentRoundsTable component (renders rounds)
    ↓
  CommandCenterWidget wrapper (displays in UI)
    ✅ VISIBLE

PLAYER_ROUNDS FLOW:
  Same as above, but:
  Service composes player_rounds via PlayerRoundRepository
    ↓
  Maps to PlayerScore objects with player names
    ↓
  TournamentRoundsTable renders as table rows
    ✅ VISIBLE (ALL 3,736 RECORDS)

SEASON STATISTICS FLOW:
  Player Detail Page
    ↓
  usePlayerDetail hook (calls getPlayerDetail)
    ↓
  PlayerService fetches player with seasonStatistics relation
    ↓
  PlayerMapper.mapPlayerDetail() builds seasonStatistics array
    ↓
  PlayerSeasonStatsCategorized component renders grid
    ✅ VISIBLE (ALL 1,225 RECORDS)
