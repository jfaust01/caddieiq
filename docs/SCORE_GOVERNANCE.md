# SCORE GOVERNANCE

Every score must be governed, traceable, and explainable.

## Score Ownership

Every score has an owner responsible for maintenance and accuracy.

## Score Formula Governance

Every score formula must be locked per version and cannot change without version increment.

## Score Validation

Before every release: formula correctness, data quality, output ranges, distributions, edge cases.

## Score Versioning

Semantic versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH) for all scores.

## Score Approval

Multi-stage approval: Data Scientist → ML Lead → CTO → CEO

## Score Retirement

Deprecated scores follow 30-day sunset with proper documentation.

## Score Calibration

Calibration adjustments tracked separately from version increments.

## Confidence Score Governance

Confidence measures data quality (not prediction accuracy). Separate from fit score.

## Score Explainability

Every score must be explainable with: lead explanation, component breakdown, form statement, venue history, risk assessment, confidence statement.

## Score Monitoring

Real-time dashboards track distributions, components, confidence, explanations, and correlation with outcomes.
