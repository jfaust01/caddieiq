================================================================================
  DATABASE SCHEMA ADDITIONS
================================================================================

New Models Added (Phase 18-19):

WeatherSnapshot
├─ Fields: tournament, round, temperature, wind, humidity, conditions
├─ Relations: Tournament, Course
├─ Indexes: (tournamentId, round), (courseId)
└─ Records: Persisted for each round

OddsQuote
├─ Fields: oddsEvent, market, bookmaker, selection, odds, probability
├─ Relations: OddsEvent, Player
├─ Unique: (oddsEventId, market, bookmakerKey, selectionSlug)
└─ Records: Multiple per selection

DfsContest
├─ Fields: externalId, tournamentId, operator, type, entryFee, salaryCap
├─ Relations: Tournament, entries, ownership
└─ Records: Per contest per tournament

DfsPlayerOwnership
├─ Fields: contestId, playerId, salary, ownership, leverage, points
├─ Relations: Contest, Player, Tournament
├─ Unique: (contestId, playerId)
└─ Records: Per player per contest
