NEW COMPONENTS (7)
================================================================================

1. TournamentTopCourseFits
   - Identifies top 10 players best suited for this course
   - Shows: Fit Score, Driving %, Approach %, Short Game %, History %
   - Files: tournament-top-course-fits.tsx (137 lines)

2. TournamentDfsValuePlays
   - Finds undervalued players with high upside
   - Shows: Salary, Value Rating, Points, Ownership, Leverage, Boom%, PPK
   - Files: tournament-dfs-value-plays.tsx (133 lines)

3. TournamentKeyStats
   - Displays tour-wide golf statistics
   - Organized by category: Driving, Approach, Putting, etc.
   - Files: tournament-key-stats.tsx (63 lines)

4. TournamentRecentWinners
   - Last 10 years of tournament winners
   - Shows: Score, Margin, World Ranking, Playoff flag
   - Files: tournament-recent-winners.tsx (98 lines)

5. TournamentCourseInformation
   - Complete course details
   - Shows: Location, Architect, Year, Par, Yardage, Grass Types
   - Files: tournament-course-information.tsx (119 lines)

6. TournamentCourseSummaryHoles
   - Hole-by-hole difficulty breakdown
   - Shows: Par Distribution, Lengths, Hardest/Easiest, Scoring Stats
   - Files: tournament-course-summary-holes.tsx (236 lines)

7. TournamentPremiumIntelligence
   - 9-section data-driven analysis
   - Sections: Executive, Trending, Specialists, Risks, Strategy, Ownership
   - Files: tournament-premium-intelligence.tsx (167 lines)
